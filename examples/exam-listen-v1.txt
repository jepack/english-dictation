# 听选关键词
Good afternoon.|正确答案：afternoon（下午好，不是 morning）
My name is Lucy.|正确答案：Lucy
I have a blue bag.|正确答案：blue
He's my friend.|正确答案：friend

# 听选应答
How are you?|应答：I'm fine, thank you.
Nice to meet you.|应答：Nice to meet you too.
What class are you in?|应答：I'm in Class One.
Goodbye.|应答：Goodbye.

# 听辨对错
It's a cat.|判断：对（是一只猫）
It's a dog.|判断：听成 cat 就算错；本句是 dog
I am nine.|判断：对（九岁）
